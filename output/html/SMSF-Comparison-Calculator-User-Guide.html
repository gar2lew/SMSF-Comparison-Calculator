1Overview

The SMSF Comparison Calculator compares a client's current superannuation setup with an SMSF scenario using the same core member details and selected growth assumptions.

What the calculator models

  • Current superannuation balance and annual salary.
  • Employer contributions based on the selected contribution rate.
  • Optional salary sacrifice as a percentage of annual salary.
  • Annual compound growth for 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, or 30 years.
  • A side-by-side comparison of projected balances, contributions, and growth.
  • A balance chart and year-by-year projection data.
Important This calculator provides illustrative estimates only. Results are not guaranteed and do not constitute financial, tax, legal, or investment advice. The calculator excludes tax, fees, insurance, contribution caps, contribution timing nuances, and market volatility.

How to use it in an appointment

Use the calculator as a conversation aid. Explain each assumption before presenting the result, keep comparable settings aligned, and focus on the difference between scenarios rather than presenting either projection as a promised outcome.

Live recalculation The calculator recalculates about 500 milliseconds after an input changes. You can also select Calculate to refresh the results immediately.

Before the appointment

  • Confirm the client's current superannuation balance.
  • Confirm annual salary and the employer contribution rate.
  • Ask whether salary sacrifice is currently used or being considered.
  • Decide which projection period is appropriate to discuss.
  • Be ready to explain the selected growth assumptions.

2Appointment quick start

Follow this sequence to create a clear and consistent comparison.

Confirm the starting pointEnter the client's current superannuation balance and annual salary in the left scenario.
Set current super assumptionsSelect the employer contribution rate, current fund growth rate, salary sacrifice setting, and projection period.
Match the SMSF inputsUse the Copy buttons to transfer the balance and salary, then align the other contribution settings for a fair comparison.
Select SMSF growthChoose the growth assumption you intend to model and explain it to the client.
Calculate and reviewSelect Calculate or wait for the live recalculation, then confirm the summary and both scenario cards.
Discuss the detailReview the chart, Projection Milestones, and full yearly breakdown when more detail is useful.
Save or shareUse Export CSV for the full data or Share to copy a link containing the current settings.
Reset when finishedSelect Reset to Example before starting a new client discussion on a shared device.
Fair comparison rule Match the starting balance, salary, employer contribution rate, salary sacrifice setting, and projection period before comparing growth assumptions. This keeps the discussion focused on the intended scenario difference.

Suggested opening explanation

"We will start with the same member details in both scenarios. The calculator then applies the selected contribution and growth assumptions so we can compare the projected balances over the same period."

Check before discussing the result

  • Both starting balances match.
  • Both annual salaries match.
  • Employer contribution rates reflect the intended comparison.
  • Salary sacrifice is either aligned or deliberately different.
  • Projection periods match.
  • The client understands that growth rates are assumptions.

Number controls

Balance and salary

Type the value directly or use the minus and plus buttons. Balance changes in $10,000 steps. Salary changes in $5,000 steps.

Salary sacrifice

Enable the option first, then type a percentage or use the minus and plus buttons to adjust it by 1 percentage point.

Copy controls

The SMSF panel includes Copy beside balance and salary. Each button copies the matching value from the Current Superannuation Setup into the SMSF scenario and triggers recalculation.

Projection period warning If the selected periods differ, the comparison uses the shorter projection period. Match the periods unless a shorter comparison is deliberate and clearly explained.

4Understanding the results

The result area moves from a high-level summary to scenario detail, then to the chart and projection table.

ResultWhat it means
Starting balanceThe starting balance used for the SMSF scenario.
Final projected balanceThe projected SMSF balance at the comparison period.
Total contributionsThe cumulative SMSF employer contributions plus salary sacrifice contributions over the comparison period.
Total earnings / growthThe projected final SMSF balance less the starting balance and total contributions.
Projected yearsThe period used for the comparison. This is the shorter period when scenario selections differ.
Winner badgeIdentifies the scenario with the higher balance at the comparison period, or shows a tie.
Final differenceThe difference between the two projected final balances.
Growth differenceThe difference between the scenarios' projected growth amounts.

Scenario cards

Each scenario shows its estimated balance after the selected years, its total change from the starting balance, and its growth percentage. The displayed growth amount includes contributions as well as investment growth, so use the summary's Total earnings / growth value when discussing investment earnings separately.

Projected yearly balance = (previous balance + annual contributions) x (1 + selected growth rate)
Explain the assumption, not only the outcome A higher selected growth rate compounds over time and can create a large difference. State the rate used in each scenario and remind the client that actual returns will vary.

Suggested result explanation

"This result shows what the two balances could look like if the selected contribution and growth assumptions continued for the chosen period. It is an illustration, not a forecast or guarantee."

5Chart and projection detail

Balance Projection chart

The line chart compares the two projected balances over time. Current Superannuation is shown in navy and SMSF with ASG + Partners is shown in gold. Labels are sampled at key years to keep longer projections readable.

  • Use the overall direction to explain compounding.
  • Point out where the distance between the lines begins to widen.
  • Hover over a point on screen to see the balance for each scenario.
  • Avoid treating a smooth line as evidence that actual returns will be consistent each year.

Projection Milestones

The default table shows Year 0, Year 1, and selected milestone years. This keeps long comparisons readable while retaining the most useful checkpoints.

Column groupWhat to look for
Current Super Balance and DifferenceProjected current-fund balance and its year-over-year movement.
SMSF Balance and DifferenceProjected SMSF balance and cumulative difference from the SMSF starting balance.
Employer ContributionAnnual contribution and running total for each scenario.
Salary SacrificeAnnual salary sacrifice and running total for each scenario when enabled.

Full yearly breakdown

Select Show full yearly breakdown to display every year in a contained scroll area. Select Show milestone view to return to the shorter view.

Full data remains available The table may show only milestones, but Export CSV always downloads the complete year-by-year dataset for the comparison period.

6Exporting, sharing, and resetting

ControlWhat it does
CalculateValidates both scenarios, calculates the projections, and refreshes the visible results.
Reset to ExampleRestores the example values, removes the calculator's saved browser state, and clears settings from the page URL.
Export CSVDownloads smsf_comparison.csv with complete year-by-year balances, differences, annual contributions, and cumulative contributions.
ShareCopies a link containing the current settings for both scenarios. Opening the link restores those values.

Browser state

The calculator saves the latest settings in the current browser. Returning to the calculator on the same browser can restore those values automatically.

Client privacy on shared devices Although the standalone calculator stores figures rather than client names, reset the calculator after each appointment on a shared device. Do not rely on browser storage as a formal client record.

Sharing checklist

  • Confirm that the assumptions are correct before selecting Share.
  • Explain that the link contains the scenario inputs.
  • Remind the recipient that results remain illustrative.
  • Use approved ASG systems and processes when sending information to a client.

CSV handling

The CSV is useful for internal review or approved follow-up analysis. Currency values are exported as numbers with two decimal places. Open the file in spreadsheet software and apply currency formatting if required.

7Best practices and troubleshooting

During the appointment

  • Confirm each input aloud.
  • Keep like-for-like settings aligned.
  • Explain why growth assumptions differ.
  • Use the chart for the overall story and the table for detail.
  • Repeat the illustrative-results disclaimer before concluding.

Avoid

  • Describing the result as guaranteed.
  • Presenting growth rates without context.
  • Comparing different periods accidentally.
  • Ignoring excluded costs and market volatility.
  • Leaving prior settings on a shared device.

Common issues

Message or issueAction
Enter a positive balance.Enter a balance greater than $0 in the affected scenario.
Enter a positive salary.Enter an annual salary greater than $0.
Salary sacrifice must be a number.Enter a numeric percentage, or disable salary sacrifice.
Salary sacrifice % must be 0-100.Enter a percentage from 0 to 100.
Copy failed.The browser could not access the clipboard. Copy the address from the browser bar instead.
Results use fewer years than expected.Check both Years of Performance selections. The comparison uses the shorter projection period.
Old values appear on return.Select Reset to Example to clear the saved browser state.

Closing explanation

"The comparison helps us discuss how different assumptions may affect the outcome. It does not include every cost, rule, or market movement, so it should be considered alongside the appropriate advice and supporting information."

Good appointment outcome: the client understands the inputs, the scenario difference, and the limits of the illustration.

Follow up if: an input is uncertain, contribution arrangements require confirmation, or the client asks for advice outside the calculator's scope.
